JD is on fire this year. JoyCode, its AI coding tool released in August, offers a limited-time free team edition with 10,000 credits every month. ## What Is JoyCode

JoyCode is an AI coding assistant launched by JD in August 2026, positioned against mainstream products like Cursor, Windsurf, and Tongyi Lingma. It is not just a code-completion plugin — it is a full AI coding workbench supporting code generation, code review, debugging, refactoring, and doc generation across the whole dev workflow.

- Team: JD Tech (JD Intelligent Technology)
- Form: desktop client (Mac/Windows) + Web IDE

- Current status: team edition limited-time free, personal edition pending

### Real Talk: 10,000 Credits Burn Fast

Let's be honest. 10,000 credits sounds like a lot, but in practice they do not last.

In my own testing, after a few rounds of conversation plus building a small utility, **over 4,000 credits were already gone**. At that burn rate, 10,000 credits probably won't survive half a month.

The reason is simple:
- JoyCode charges by token, not by "call"
- Each conversation can consume a few hundred to over a thousand credits
- Complex code generation and long-context conversations burn even faster
- Free credits do not top up infinitely — once used up, you wait for next month

### Money-Saving Tips

1. **Use JoyAI for daily work**: the in-house model costs no credits and covers everyday dev
2. **Save paid models for hard tasks**: reserve GLM-5.1, DeepSeek, etc. for when you really need them
3. **Don't switch models frequently**: each switch may reset context and waste credits
4. **Keep conversations short**: state your requirement clearly in one shot instead of going back and forth

## Versus Cursor and Windsurf

A lot of people compare JoyCode with Cursor. After two weeks of use, here is the verdict:

### Strengths

1. **Free**: Cursor's team edition is $15/month; Windsurf's free tier is capped. JoyCode is currently free, and the credits cover daily use
2. **Domestic model lineup**: supports GLM, DeepSeek, Kimi, Doubao — a better experience with Chinese models than Copilot
3. **JoyAI in-house model costs nothing**: everyday dev basically never touches the free quota
4. **Strong Chinese support**: built by the JD team, so Chinese code comments and docs are understood more accurately
5. **Works on domestic networks**: servers are in China, no VPN needed

### Weaknesses

1. **Ecosystem is not mature**: Cursor has 1M+ users and a rich plugin ecosystem; JoyCode is new and has few plugins
2. **Average stability**: occasional hiccups; code-completion latency is 2–3 seconds higher than Cursor
3. **Features still missing**: no Cursor-style enhanced Tab autocomplete, no multi-file editing
4. **Thin docs**: official docs update slowly; some features you have to figure out yourself

### Who Should Use It

- Individual developers and students on a tight budget
- Developers who mainly use domestic models
- Users who don't want to juggle a VPN
- Programmers who like freebies

### Who Should Skip It

- Heavy Cursor users for whom migration is costly
- Teams that need advanced AI features (multi-file editing, MCP plugins, etc.)
- Commercial projects that demand maximum stability

## FAQ

**Q1: Is JoyCode really free? Will it suddenly start charging?**
A1: The limited-time free team edition is official policy. But we all know JD's playbook — subsidize first to grab users, then charge. Ride the free wave now and decide on paid later when the notice comes.

**Q2: When does the personal edition ship?**
A2: Only the team edition is free right now. The personal edition may open later or stay paid-only. Register for the team edition first and migrate when the personal one is available.

**Q3: What if I run out of credits?**
A3: Wait for next month's refresh (10,000/month), invite team members (may earn bonus credits), or wait for official credit campaigns.

**Q4: Which is better, JoyAI or GLM-5?**
A4: For daily dev use JoyAI — it costs no credits. For complex tasks use GLM-5.1; Zhipu's models have stronger logic. Switching between them is easy with no reconfiguration.

**Q5: Can JoyCode be used in commercial projects?**
A5: Yes. The free tier has no code-ownership restrictions. But keep an eye on policy changes so you are not caught off guard when pricing arrives.
