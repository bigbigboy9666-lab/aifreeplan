# 5 Free AI Subtitle Generator Tools Compared (2026): CapCut vs Descript vs VEED

Video needs subtitles. In 2026, AI subtitle tools are mature. I tested 12 tools and picked the 5 best.

---

## 1. CapCut — Best Free Option

**Free tier**: Completely free

CapCut is the best choice for social media creators. AI subtitle generation is completely free, supporting 100+ languages with auto word-by-word animation effects.

**Highlights**:
- Completely free, no watermark
- 100+ languages including Chinese and English
- Word-by-word animation, perfect for short videos
- Built-in video editing

**Drawbacks**:
- Pro features require payment
- Limited batch processing

**Best for**: TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Reels creators.

---

## 2. VEED — Easiest to Use

**Free tier**: Basic features free

VEED's AI subtitle generator is web-based, zero installation. Free tier supports basic subtitle generation.

**Highlights**:
- Web-based operation
- 125+ languages
- Dynamic subtitle effects
- Simple and intuitive

**Drawbacks**:
- Watermark on free tier
- Duration limits on free tier

**Best for**: Light users who don't want to install software.

---

## 3. Descript — Edit Video Like Text

**Free tier**: Basic features free

Descript's unique feature: edit video by editing text. Change the subtitle text, and the video updates automatically.

**Highlights**:
- Unique text-to-video editing experience
- AI subtitles + editing in one tool
- Great for long videos and podcasts

**Drawbacks**:
- Limited free tier
- Steeper learning curve

**Best for**: Podcast creators, long video editors.

---

## 4. Maestra — Best Multilingual

**Free tier**: Basic features free

Maestra supports 130+ languages, the strongest multilingual subtitle generation.

**Highlights**:
- 130+ languages
- High-accuracy subtitles
- Multiple export formats

**Drawbacks**:
- Limited free tier
- Registration required

**Best for**: Multilingual content creators.

---

## 5. YouTube Studio — Most Convenient

**Free tier**: Completely free

YouTube's built-in subtitle generation. Upload a video and it auto-generates subtitles.

**Highlights**:
- Completely free
- Deep YouTube integration
- High auto-subtitle accuracy

**Drawbacks**:
- YouTube-only
- Limited editing features

**Best for**: YouTube creators, most convenient option.
