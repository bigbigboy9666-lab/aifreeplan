# NotebookLM Complete Free Guide: Google's AI Research Tool with 50 Sources, Podcast & Video Generation — All Free

NotebookLM is Google's free AI research tool. Upload up to 50 sources, generate podcasts and video narrations.

---

## What is NotebookLM

NotebookLM is Google's AI research assistant. You can upload documents, web pages, PDFs as knowledge sources, then let AI summarize, question, and generate podcasts.

In July 2026, NotebookLM was rebranded to Gemini Notebook, but functionality remains unchanged and still completely free.

---

## Core Features

**Knowledge sources**:
- Upload up to 50 sources (PDFs, web pages, Google Docs, etc.)
- AI answers questions based on these sources
- Every answer includes source citations

**Podcast generation**:
- One-click turn your materials into a two-host podcast
- Two AI hosts discuss your content
- Completely free

**Video narration**:
- Generate video-format narrations
- Great for learning and sharing

**Q&A**:
- Free-form questioning based on your materials
- AI answers with source citations

---

## How to Use

1. Visit notebooklm.google (now gemini.google.com/notebook)
2. Log in with Google account
3. Create a Notebook, upload sources (up to 50)
4. Start asking questions or generating podcasts
