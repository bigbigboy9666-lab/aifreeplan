AI Writing Tools Free Tier Comparison: 10 Popular Tools Reviewed in 2026

2026 has too many AI writing tools. I compared 10 mainstream tools’ free tiers.

1. Writesonic — Beginner Friendly

Free tier: 25,000 words/month

Writesonic is the most beginner-friendly AI writing tool with built-in SEO features.

Highlights:

  • 25,000 words/month free
  • Built-in SEO optimization
  • 80+ writing templates
  • Beginner friendly

Drawbacks:

  • Advanced features require payment
  • Free tier insufficient for heavy users

2. Jasper — Professional Choice

Free tier: 5,000 words/month

Jasper is a professional writing tool with powerful features but limited free tier.

Highlights:

  • Most comprehensive features
  • Multi-language support
  • Team collaboration

Drawbacks:

  • Low free tier (5,000 words/month)
  • Expensive

3. Copy.ai — Marketing Focused

Free tier: 2,000 words/month

Copy.ai focuses on marketing content generation.

Highlights:

  • Rich marketing templates
  • Social media copy support
  • Clean interface

4. ChatGPT (OpenAI)

Free tier: GPT-3.5 free, GPT-4o limited

OpenAI’s ChatGPT is the most versatile AI writing tool.

Highlights:

  • GPT-3.5 completely free
  • Strong general writing
  • Multi-language support

5. Tongyi Qianwen (Alibaba)

Free tier: Qwen Studio completely free

Alibaba’s Qwen Studio is completely free.

Highlights:

  • Completely free
  • Excellent Chinese support
  • Multimodal capabilities

6. ERNIE Bot (Baidu)

Free tier: Completely free

Baidu’s ERNIE Bot is completely free.

Highlights:

  • Completely free
  • Good Chinese understanding
  • Baidu ecosystem integration
