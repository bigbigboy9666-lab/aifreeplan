AI Music & Audio Generation Free Tier Comparison: 8 Top Tools in 2026

2026 has many AI music and audio tools. I compared 8 mainstream tools’ free tiers.

1. Suno AI — Most Free

Free tier: 50 credits/day (~10 songs)

Suno has the most generous free tier among AI music tools.

Highlights:

  • 50 credits/day
  • Full song structure (verse, chorus, bridge)
  • Great for complete song creation

Drawbacks:

  • No commercial rights on free tier
  • Average sound quality

2. Udio — Atmospheric

Free tier: Limited free

Udio has fewer free credits but excellent sound quality.

Highlights:

  • Excellent sound quality
  • Strong atmospheric feel
  • Great for background music

3. ElevenLabs — Best TTS

Free tier: $10/month

ElevenLabs is the best AI voice synthesis tool.

Highlights:

  • Best-in-class sound quality
  • Multi-language support
  • Voice cloning

4. Stable Audio (Stability AI)

Free tier: 20 songs/month

Stability AI’s music generation tool.

Highlights:

  • 20 songs/month free
  • Great for sound effects
  • Integrates with Stable Diffusion ecosystem

5. Mubert — Background Music

Free tier: Limited free

Mubert focuses on AI-generated background music.

Highlights:

  • Great for video BGM
  • Customizable styles
  • Free tier available

6. Soundraw — Royalty-Free

Free tier: Limited free

Soundraw provides royalty-free AI music.

Highlights:

  • Royalty-free
  • Great for content creators
  • Customizable length and style

7. AIVA — Classical/Score

Free tier: Limited free

AIVA excels at classical music and film scores.

Highlights:

  • Strong classical music
  • Film scoring
  • MIDI export

8. Voicemod — Real-time Voice

Free tier: Basic features free

Voicemod focuses on real-time voice changing.

Highlights:

  • Real-time voice changing
  • Gaming/streaming use
  • Basic features free
