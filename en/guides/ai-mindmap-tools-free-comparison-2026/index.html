AI Mind Map Tools Free Tier Comparison: Deep Review of 8 Mainstream Tools in 2026

Mind maps are efficient tools for organizing thoughts, brainstorming, and knowledge management. With AI technology development, more mind map tools now integrate AI generation — just input a topic and AI generates the complete structure. But free tiers vary wildly, from completely free to only a few AI generations per day.

This article compares free versions of 8 mainstream AI mind map tools (including domestic and international products), covering AI generation count, node limits, export formats, and collaboration features.

1. Baidu Naotu — Completely Free, Most Generous

Baidu Naotu (naotu.baidu.com) is the most established free mind map tool in China.

Free details:

  • Map count: Unlimited creation
  • Node count: Unlimited
  • Export: Markdown, PNG, JPG, PDF
  • Cloud sync: Free
  • Collaboration: Multi-user real-time editing

Pros and Cons:

  • Biggest advantage is completely free — no membership, no limits, no ads
  • Supports Markdown import/export, great for developers
  • Interface is plain, no AI generation

Best for: Users with zero budget who only need basic mind map functionality.

2. GitMind — 5 Daily AI Generations, Most Generous Free Tier

GitMind (gitmind.cn) is a comprehensive AI mind map tool.

Free details:

  • AI generations: 5 free per day
  • Map count: Unlimited
  • Node limit: ~200 nodes per map
  • Export: PNG, PDF, Text
  • Cloud storage: 5GB free

Pros and Cons:

  • 5 daily AI generations is very high compared to competitors
  • Beautiful interface, multi-platform support
  • Free tier limits to ~200 nodes

Best for: Students and professionals who need frequent AI generation.

3. XMind AI — Professional Grade, 3 Daily AI Generations

XMind is one of the world’s most popular mind mapping software, launched AI-integrated XMind AI in 2026.

Free details:

  • AI generations: 3 free per day
  • AI summary: 3 per day
  • Map count: Unlimited
  • Node count: Unlimited
  • Export: Free tier can export PNG, TXT; full formats require payment

Pros and Cons:

  • Professional chart design and unlimited nodes
  • High-quality AI generation
  • Free tier export formats limited

Best for: Professional users who value quality and aesthetics.

4. Miro AI — Whiteboard Collaboration, Limited Free Tier

Miro is a leading global online whiteboard tool, launched Miro AI in 2026.

Free details:

  • AI generations: 3 per month
  • Whiteboards: 3 unlimited edit whiteboards
  • Node limit: ~200 elements per board
  • Export: PNG
  • Collaboration: Up to 3 simultaneous editors

Pros and Cons:

  • Powerful team collaboration and whiteboard freedom
  • Very limited AI — only 3 per month
  • Free tier limited to 3 whiteboards

Best for: Small teams (under 3 people) needing collaboration whiteboard features.

5. Whimsical AI — Clean Design, Moderate AI Quota

Whimsical is known for its clean design, launched Whimsical AI in 2026 for one-click flowcharts, wireframes, and mind maps.

Free details:

  • AI generations: 5 AI boards per month
  • Board count: 5 free boards
  • Node limit: ~100 elements per board
  • Export: PNG, SVG
  • Collaboration: Up to 3 simultaneous editors

Pros and Cons:

  • Best design aesthetics in the category
  • Auto-applies unified color schemes and layouts
  • Free tier has many limitations

Best for: Light users who value design aesthetics.

6. Boardmix — All-in-One Domestic Option

Boardmix is an AI-powered whiteboard tool from ByteDance.

Free details:

  • AI generations: 10 per month
  • Map count: Unlimited
  • Node limit: ~300 nodes
  • Export: PNG, PDF
  • Collaboration: Up to 5 people

Pros:

  • Domestic tool, stable access
  • Integrates mind maps, flowcharts, whiteboard collaboration
  • Multiple AI features built-in

Best for: Chinese users needing an all-in-one whiteboard tool.
