Seko, updream & Xiaoyunque: Free-Tier Comparison of 3 AI Short-Drama / Video Creation Agents (2026)

In 2026, "one line to a finished video" AI short-drama / video-creation agents started appearing in bulk. The ai-bot.cn latest-AI-projects list now carries three names at once: SenseTime's Seko, Bilibili's updream, and ByteDance Jianying's Xiaoyunque ("Little Lark"). None of these is a "generate one shot" tool — they are full-pipeline agents covering script, storyboards, character consistency, lip-sync, and export, aimed at short-drama production, digital-human talking-heads, and hit-video replication. The difference is how generous the free tier is and how low the barrier. Below is a full breakdown of each one's zero-cost start, daily free quota, signup bonus, and entry price (numbers pulled 2026-10-08 from the official sites and the ai-bot.cn entries).

Seko: multi-episode production without "face-swap" breaks

Seko is SenseTime's "one-person film crew" AI director system — the first agent that stresses create-and-edit-in-one plus multi-episode generation. Its two hardest features are SekoIDX (characters stay consistent across shots and episodes, no face-swap glitches) and SekoTalk (multi-person lip-sync, up to 60s per shot, 60 built-in timbres). It aggregates Seedance 2.0, Kling, Vidu, Wan, PixVerse, and Hailuo and dispatches them on demand, so you are not locked to one model. Free side: the Standard tier's first purchase is about CNY 39/month for 500 credits, Premium about CNY 195/month for 3,000 credits (up to 50 continuous episodes), and Enterprise supports up to 100 episodes; free members get a daily-login credit bonus. As of 2026-07 it has 1,000,000+ creators and 1,300 enterprise customers. Seko 3.0 (shipped at WAIC 2026-07) adds a canvas Agent, a skill community, and multi-language export. Best for teams making serialized short-dramas / comic-dramas where characters must stay consistent.

updream: 5,000 free credits, but you need a Bilibili account + invite code

updream is Bilibili's one-stop AI video workbench for professional creators, built on long-memory Agent, a skill library, and a boundless canvas; the process supports non-linear, retroactive edits. It is wired into the Bilibili ecosystem, so finished works can be posted to Bilibili in one click. The most tangible free offer: new users get 5,000 credits on signup, then top up by credit pack (CNY 6 / 600, CNY 30 / 3,000, CNY 98 / 9,800, CNY 198 / 19,800, CNY 328 / 32,800, CNY 648 / 64,800). The catch is that it is still in beta — signup requires a Bilibili account plus an invite code, so you cannot just walk in. Best for creators already in the Bilibili ecosystem who want a professional workbench for serialized short-dramas / animation concepts / pre-visualization.

Xiaoyunque: 120 credits a day — the most generous zero-barrier freebie

Xiaoyunque ("Little Lark") is a content-creation Agent from ByteDance's Jianying/CapCut team, the "one line to a hit" experience, available as an Android app and web app. It runs on Doubao, DeepSeek, and in-house Seedance 2.0/2.5 video and Seedream 5.0 image models. Its free strategy is the friendliest of the three for newcomers: 120 free credits on every daily login, roughly 12 smart videos/day, and core features are currently free (limited-time). Capabilities include 15-60s short videos, 200+ commercial-usable digital humans across 19 languages and dialects, hit-video replication (paste a link, it auto-replicates the style), AI design (batch posters / product images from one prompt), and "photo talking". Best for creators, e-commerce marketers, and educators who need high-frequency, low-cost, batch short-video output.

Which to pick (starting at zero cost)

  • Want unlimited-ish daily free short videos / digital-human talking-heads: pick Xiaoyunque — 120 credits/day (~12 videos) + core features free, no invite code.
  • Want serialized short-dramas where characters must not change across episodes: pick Seko — SekoIDX + multi-episode (Premium 50, Enterprise 100) + SekoTalk, entry from CNY 39/mo.
  • Already in the Bilibili ecosystem and want a pro workbench: pick updream — 5,000 credits on signup + one-click Bilibili posting, but beta invite code required.

One universal tip: all three quotas are shifting fast (Xiaoyunque is "limited-time free", updream is "beta"), so once you register, run through the model tiers and digital-human quotas you care about early before policy changes.

Frequently asked questions

Q: Which of the three has the best free tier?
It depends on use. For pure free daily short-video / digital-human output, Xiaoyunque's 120 credits/day (~12 videos) wins. For a one-time large credit pool, updream gives 5,000 credits on signup. For serialized short-dramas and you are okay paying to start, Seko from CNY 39/mo (500 credits). All three let you open an account for free.
Q: Does updream really require a Bilibili account and an invite code?
Yes. updream is currently in beta; signup needs a Bilibili account plus an invite code. That is also the gate on its "5,000 free credits on signup". Without an invite code, start with Xiaoyunque or Seko.
Q: Can the generated videos / short-dramas be used commercially?
Xiaoyunque offers 200+ commercial-usable digital humans; Seko paid tiers usually include commercial licensing, with Enterprise more complete; updream centers on posting to Bilibili. Exact commercial scope follows each site's / membership's terms — confirm before publishing.
Q: Do I need a powerful computer?
No. All three are cloud platforms (Xiaoyunque also has an Android app). Open the site in a browser and generation runs in the cloud — no local GPU or heavy specs.

Bottom line

In 2026 the "free window" in this lane is obvious: Xiaoyunque's 120 credits/day + limited-time free is the best free tier for everyday creators; updream's 5,000-credit signup bonus is the largest one-time new-user pool (but needs a Bilibili account + invite code); and Seko from CNY 39/mo (500 credits) is the pro pick for multi-episode production without character breaks. Recommended path: farm Xiaoyunque for volume on day one, then use Seko or updream for long serialized projects or Bilibili distribution.
