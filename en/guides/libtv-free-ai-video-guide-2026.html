LibTV Free Guide: All-in-One AI Video Creation Platform

LibTV is an all-in-one AI video creation platform launched by LiblibAI, a well-known Chinese AI model community. Unlike most "one-click video generation" tools on the market, LibTV focuses on infinite canvas workflow and Agent automation capabilities, supporting the complete process from script analysis, character design, storyboard planning to video generation.

1. Core Features of LibTV

A. Infinite Canvas Workflow (Human Creator Entry)

LibTV's infinite canvas supports freely placing text, images, videos, audio, and scripts as nodes, building complex creative projects through drag-and-drop and connection. The entire creative process can be completed on a single canvas without switching between multiple tools.

  • Creative Empowerment: Script analysis, script optimization, one-click scene breakdown, multi-panel plot deduction preview
  • Professional Control: 20+ exclusive AI capabilities including character three-view and 360-degree presentation, multi-camera 9-panel grid, focus close-up, lighting control, camera control, master-level cinematography
  • Full-Process Closed Loop: From script editing to storyboard management to final preview — all in one place

B. Agent Automation (Skill Interface)

LibTV is the industry's first professional video platform with dual entry points for both human creators and AI Agents. Through the Skill interface, Agents can automatically call platform capabilities:

  • One-Click Short Drama/Manga Generation: Input a creative description, and the Agent automatically generates scripts, characters, storyboards, videos, sound effects, and editing — delivering a complete work up to 5 minutes long
  • Reference Video Recreation: Input a video URL, and the Agent parses the original's camera language and style features to generate a new work in the same style
  • Music Video Generation: Upload a music file, the AI analyzes rhythm and emotional progression, and automatically generates matching visual narratives edited into a finished product
  • Product Website Video Generation: Input a product website URL, the Agent automatically extracts product selling points, designs display pacing, and generates professional promotional videos

2. Integrated Models

LibTV integrates 15+ top-tier AI models, including Veo 3.1, Sora 2 Pro, Kling 3.0, Jimeng 4.0, Midjourney, Flux, and more. Users can switch between different models within the same platform and choose the best generation solution based on their needs.

4. How to Use

  1. Visit the website: Go to https://www.liblib.tv/, register and log in with your LiblibAI account
  2. Create a project: Double-click blank space in the infinite canvas, choose from story script, character three-view, or audio/video generation types
  3. Organize workflow: Drag text, images, videos, audio, and script nodes onto the canvas, arrange and connect them freely
  4. Call capabilities: Select nodes and use 20+ professional features from the top toolbar to set style markers and other parameters
  5. Preview and iterate: One-click generation with quick preview on the canvas, iterate until satisfied

5. Use Cases

  • AI Film Production: Use the infinite canvas to coordinate scripts, storyboards, shots, and editing for full-length or short films
  • AI TVC Ads: Brands and advertising teams quickly generate product showcase videos, supporting direct website link generation or reference video recreation
  • AI Short Dramas/Manga: Generate complete short dramas up to 5 minutes with one sentence, automatically handling scripts, characters, storyboards, video, sound effects, and editing
  • Music MV Creation: Upload music files, the AI analyzes rhythm and emotion, and automatically generates matching visual narratives
