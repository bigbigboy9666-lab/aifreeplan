AI Writing Tools Free Tier Comparison: 10 Popular Tools Reviewed in 2026

\n

The AI writing tool market in 2026 is extremely crowded. From general-purpose writing to web novel creation, academic papers, and official document drafting, nearly every niche has specialized tools. However, most tools adopt a freemium model, and the real value of free tiers varies dramatically — some are genuinely useful, while others are merely trials.

\n

This article provides a hands-on comparison of the free tiers of 10 mainstream AI writing tools, covering free quotas, monthly word limits, feature restrictions, and export capabilities, helping you find the best free writing solution for your needs.

1. Meta Writer (Mitã) — Most Generous Free Tier

\n

Meta Writer is one of the earliest AI writing platforms in China. The free tier provides approximately 50,000 words per month (~1,600 words/day). It supports documents, emails, reports, and social media copy. Free users can export to Word and PDF formats without watermarks.

\n

Free highlights: No ads, Markdown support, grammar correction and rewriting features, collaboration for up to 3 users.

\n

Limitations: Advanced models (long-form generation, deep polishing) require paid upgrades. Custom brand glossaries are unavailable on the free tier.

\n

2. Yuling Writer — Hidden Gem with Truly Free Plan

\n

Yuling Writer is a rising AI writing tool in 2026 with a genuinely free strategy. It offers approximately 30,000 words per month for free, with no watermarks and no export restrictions. It supports both Chinese and English writing, making it ideal for cross-border e-commerce copy and social media content.

\n

Free highlights: Completely free, no watermark exports, bilingual support, 10+ writing templates.

\n

Limitations: Newer platform, model capability lags behind top products, long-form coherence is average.

\n

3. Biling AI — Most Template-Rich Practical Tool

\n

Biling AI provides 300 free credits daily. At 1 credit = 10 words, that is approximately 15,000 words per month. It features 200+ writing templates covering e-commerce copy, marketing emails, social media posts, and business plans. Free users can export basic content.

\n

Free highlights: Largest template library (200+), batch generation support, content optimization suggestions.

\n

Limitations: 300-credit daily cap, advanced templates require payment, long articles (5000+ words) must be split.

\n

4. Guangsu Writer — Best for Long-Form Creators

\n

Guangsu Writer offers 200 free credits daily (~10,000 words/month). Its standout feature is continuous long-form generation — it can produce coherent content of 3,000+ words in a single pass, making it ideal for novels and reports. Free users can export to Markdown.

\n

Free highlights: Strong long-form generation, story mode, automatic outline generation.

\n

Limitations: 200-credit daily cap, advanced styles (academic, official documents) require payment.

\n

5. Xiaohuanxiong — Best for Knowledge-Base Writing

\n

Xiaohuanxiong provides 150 free credits daily (~7,500 words/month). Its core selling point is the AI knowledge base feature — upload your own documents as reference material, and the AI generates content based on your materials. The free tier supports 3 knowledge bases with up to 50 documents each.

\n

Free highlights: AI knowledge base support, content citation annotations, ideal for enterprise document generation.

\n

Limitations: Limited to 3 knowledge bases, advanced analytics require paid plan.

\n

6. GetDraft AI — Expert Collaboration Mode

\n

GetDraft AI, launched by the Dedao team, offers approximately 20,000 words per month for free. Its unique feature is multi-expert collaboration — multiple AI roles (editor, fact-checker, SEO expert) can simultaneously optimize the same article. The free tier supports 2 expert roles working together.

\n

Free highlights: Multi-expert collaboration, SEO optimization suggestions, content compliance checks.

\n

Limitations: Free tier limited to 2 expert roles, advanced SEO data requires payment.

\n

7. Xieshou Bang — Entry-Level Free Tool

\n

Xieshou Bang provides 100 free credits daily (~5,000 words/month). It is suitable for short-form daily writing such as social media posts, WeChat moments, and Xiaohongshu notes. Free users can export with a platform watermark.

\n

Free highlights: Simple operation, beginner-friendly, social media templates.

\n

Limitations: Watermarked exports, low daily quota, no long-form support.

\n

8. Zhi Writer — Lightweight Option

\n

Zhi Writer offers 50 free credits daily (~2,500 words/month). Positioned as a lightweight writing assistant, it suits occasional writers. The free tier supports basic text generation and simple polishing.

\n

Free highlights: Clean interface, fast startup, suitable for quick short-text generation.

\n

Limitations: Very low quota, few templates (only 10), no export functionality.

\n

9. WaWa Writer — Web Novel Specialist

\n

WaWa Writer focuses on web novel creation, offering approximately 3,000 words per month for free. Its specialty is character management and plot simulation — you can set up character relationships and world-building, and the AI generates coherent novel chapters based on these settings. The free tier supports basic character management.

\n

Free highlights: Web novel dedicated, character setting management, plot coherence checking.

\n

Limitations: Low free quota, advanced models (deep plot simulation) require payment.

\n

10. Mole Writer — Notes-Integrated Writing Tool

\n

Mole Writer provides 50 free credits daily (~2,500 words/month). Its unique approach combines note-taking with AI writing — you can invoke AI to expand, summarize, or rewrite text directly within your notes. The free tier supports basic bidirectional linking note functionality.

\n

Free highlights: Notes + writing integrated, bidirectional linking support, ideal for knowledge management.

\n

Limitations: Low quota, advanced search and tagging require payment.

FAQ

\n

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Are the free tiers of these tools really free to use indefinitely?
Most tools offer genuinely free tiers with usage or feature limits. However, AI tool pricing policies can change frequently due to intense market competition. Check the current free policy before committing to heavy usage.
Q: Can I use content generated on free tiers for commercial purposes?
Most tools allow commercial use, but confirm in each tool terms. Meta Writer and Yuling Writer explicitly permit commercial exports.
Q: Can I combine multiple tools for better results?
Absolutely. Many professional writers combine tools — for example, using Biling AI for drafts, Meta Writer for polishing, and Xiaohuanxiong for knowledge-base-backed professional review.
