1. Meta Writer — Most Generous Free Tier

Meta Writer is one of China’s earliest AI writing tool platforms. Free tier offers ~50,000 words/month, about 1,600 words/day.

Free highlights:

  • No ads, Markdown support
  • Grammar correction and rewriting features
  • Multi-user collaboration (up to 3)

Limitations:

  • Advanced models require paid upgrade
  • No custom brand word library in free tier
